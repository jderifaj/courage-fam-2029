Welcome to our family page! We're so excited to be part of the 2029 NC Courage Academy team this season.

## About Us

We're a family of four (plus one very spoiled dog) who love spending our weekends outdoors — hiking, camping, and of course, chasing a soccer ball around the backyard. When we're not at practice or games, you can find us trying out new recipes in the kitchen or having a family movie night.

## Hobbies & Interests

- Hiking and camping at state parks
- Board game nights (Emma always wins)
- Baking — we make a mean batch of cookies for the team
- Watching NWSL games together on the weekend

## Pets

Meet Biscuit, our golden retriever who thinks every soccer practice is actually for him. He's the unofficial team mascot.

## What the Parents Do

Mom works as a nurse at the local hospital, and Dad is a software engineer who somehow still finds time to help coach Emma's recreational team on the side.

## Get to Know Emma

Emma has been playing soccer since she was five and loves being a midfielder because she "gets to be everywhere on the field." Outside of soccer, she enjoys drawing, reading mystery novels, and teaching Biscuit new tricks (with mixed success).

We can't wait to get to know all the other families this season — see you on the sidelines!
